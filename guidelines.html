01 — Guidelines

How to use this kit

These guidelines apply to every Plainsight deliverable: pitch decks, proposals, blog posts, dashboards, social posts, internal docs. If you're about to publish something that carries our name, this is where you start.

The six rules

If you remember nothing else, remember these.

  • Blue is the voice, orange is the shout. Plainsight Blue carries roughly 90% of the pigment on any page. Orange is reserved for one thing per view — the primary CTA, the callout number, the single highlighted bar.
  • White (or cream) backgrounds first. Reverse-out blue backgrounds are for one hero slide or one section divider — not whole decks.
  • Two type families, never more. Titillium Web for headings and labels. Epilogue for body copy and quotes. No third font.
  • No new hues. If you need more than two colors for a chart, extend with blue tints — never introduce a green, a teal or a purple.
  • The triangle and the rocket are ours. Thin triangles and rocket silhouettes are the signature graphic motifs. Use them sparingly — they lose meaning if every page has one.
  • Sentence case in body, Title Case in headings. All-caps is reserved for the eyebrow labels and section pills.

The five-minute checklist

Run through this before you send anything to a client.

  • Is Plainsight Blue (#000075) the dominant color? Body text, headings, primary fills — all blue, not black.
  • Is Orange (#d5693a) used at most for one accent element per view? If everything is orange, nothing is.
  • Are Titillium Web and Epilogue both present? Fall back to Arial only if one truly isn't available.
  • Is the background white, cream or mist? Dark blue backgrounds only for one hero or divider.
  • Have I avoided introducing new hues? Greens, teals, purples — none of them are Plainsight.
  • Does any CTA or highlight have enough contrast? Orange on white needs ≥18 pt, or white on blue.

When in doubt

Look at plainsight.pro. The website is the canonical reference for how the brand looks in practice — when this kit and the website disagree, the website wins, and please open a ticket so we can fix this kit.
