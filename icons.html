10 — Icons

Iconography

We use a single icon family across all surfaces. We pick an open, well-maintained outline set rather than a paid bespoke one — the cost-to-coverage ratio is better and the visual consistency is identical.

Recommended set

Tabler Icons — outline style, 5800+ icons, MIT-licensed, free for commercial use. The line weight matches our brand and pairs well with Titillium Web.

  • Outline, not filled. Always the outline variant — it matches the thin triangle motif.
  • 1.5px stroke at 24px. Anything thicker reads as a logo, anything thinner disappears in print.
  • Plainsight Blue on light, white on dark. Orange icons only when they accompany an orange CTA or highlight.
  • One size per layout. Don't mix 16px and 32px icons in the same row. Pick one, repeat it.
  • No coloured backgrounds behind every icon. Tinted circle/square backplates only for the rare emphasized icon — never as the default treatment.

The exception: domain icons

The website uses bespoke illustrations for technologies (Azure, Databricks, Power BI, etc.) and offerings (Strategy, Projects, Experts). These are not icons — they're illustrations. They follow their own rules and live in the website's CMS. Don't try to recreate them in Tabler.
