Color pairing rules

These rules apply across all media — slides, web, print.

  • Navy and off-white alternate as backgrounds; white is reserved for cards and content containers.
  • Orange is always accent. Never use it as a full-section or full-slide background fill.
  • Text on navy = white or light navy (#b0b0d0). Text on cream/off-white = navy or dark text (#1a1a3e).
  • All pairings must meet WCAG AA contrast. Navy on cream exceeds 7:1 — safe at any size.

Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast notes for our two brand colors.

  • Plainsight Blue on white passes AA and AAA for body text — safe at any size.
  • Plainsight Orange on white passes AA only at 18pt regular or 14pt bold and larger. Don't use it for body copy.
  • White on Plainsight Blue passes AAA — safe for hero text and reverse-out sections.
  • White on Plainsight Orange passes AA at large sizes (18pt+) — fine for CTAs, not for small captions.
