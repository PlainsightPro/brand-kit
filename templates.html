LinkedIn carousel specs

Every Plainsight carousel follows a fixed canvas, two template variants, and a 7-slide narrative arc.

Canvas

1080 × 1350 px (4:5 ratio). Margins: 72 px all sides. Content width: 936 px.

Two templates

Template A — "Warm Accent"

Off-white background, blue triangles both corners, thick orange accent lines (160 px, 6 px), navy punchline cards (24 px radius), orange closing cards (20 px radius).

Template B — "Bold Statements"

Off-white background, bottom-only blue triangles, oversized typography, orange underline bars (180 px, 7 px) below titles, prominent statement cards.

7-slide narrative arc

SlidePurposeContent density
1 — HookStrong statement creating curiosity or tensionMax 3 lines + subtitle (max 2 lines)
2 — RecognitionWhat people commonly see ("I recognise this")1 title + 3-5 observation lines + 1 punchline
3 — ReframeReveal the deeper issue, shift perspective1 title + 1 intro + 3-5 bullets + 1 closing
4 — Trade-offTwo opposing sides / the tension1 title + 2 contrast boxes (3-4 bullets each) + 1 closing
5 — PracticalActionable guardrails or steps1 title + 1 intro + 3-5 cards (title + desc each)
6 — PerspectivePlainsight point of view1 title + 3-4 statement cards
7 — ClosingReflective statement (not promotional)1 statement (max 3 lines) + 1 subtext line

Slide furniture

  • Position dots: Top-right, 5 px radius, 18 px spacing. Active = orange, inactive = #c0c0c0.
  • Slide counter: Bottom-left, "1 / 7" format, muted blue (#8888bb), 14pt.
  • Topic badge: Top-left pill, uppercase. Navy bg (Template A) or orange bg (Template B).
  • Logo: Bottom-right, 150 px wide, blue version.

Cards & accents

  • Rounded corners: 16-24 px radius throughout.
  • Card fills: Navy for punchline cards, orange for closing cards, white for content cards.
  • Orange left accent bars: 6-8 px wide, rounded ends.
  • Orange bullets: Filled circle, 5 px radius, 24 px text indent.
  • Number badges: 42-50 px circle, orange or navy fill, white text.

Website components

Layout rules and component patterns for the Plainsight website (Next.js / Tailwind).

Layout rules

  • Container: max-w-site (1280 px) + mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8.
  • Content column: max-w-content (720 px) for text-heavy pages.
  • Section rhythm: py-16 md:py-24 lg:py-32. Alternate bg-surface / bg-surface-muted.
  • Header: Sticky, bg-surface/95 backdrop-blur-sm, h-16, z-50.
  • Footer: bg-brand-500 (navy), white text hierarchy.

Component patterns

  • Buttons: 4 variants — primary (orange), secondary (navy), outline, ghost. CTA buttons: rounded-full uppercase tracking-wider font-semibold.
  • Cards: rounded-card (12 px), border-surface-border, shadow-card at rest / shadow-card-hover on hover. Title hover = accent-500.
  • Tags/Badges: rounded-full pill shape. Variants: default (cream), brand (navy tint), accent (orange tint).
  • Section wrappers: Use SectionContainer component with narrow and background props.

Presentation rules

Slide design rules when creating PowerPoint decks for Plainsight.

  • Background: Off-white (#fcf8f3) for content slides, navy (#000075) for section dividers and closing.
  • Font: Inter or Titillium Web, bold uppercase for headings.
  • Orange accent lines and bullets, never orange backgrounds.
  • Triangle decorations at corners — subtle, low opacity.
  • Logo on title and closing slides.
  • Maximum 2 font weights per slide.
  • One idea per slide, generous whitespace.
