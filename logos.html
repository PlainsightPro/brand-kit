03 — Logos

Logo

The Plainsight wordmark has two versions: blue for light backgrounds, white for dark backgrounds. Use the SVG when you can — it scales without losing sharpness. PNG is for tools that don't accept SVG (some legacy Office templates, some Office365 environments).

Variants

Four contexts cover almost everything we do.

Primary — on white
Reverse — on Plainsight Blue
Acceptable — on cream
Acceptable — on mist

Logo rules

Treat the wordmark with respect. It's the first thing people see.

Do

  • Keep clear space of at least the logo's "P" on every side.
  • Use the SVG file for digital, the PNG for tools that can't render SVG.
  • Minimum width: 80px on screen, 25mm in print.
  • Use the white version on Plainsight Blue or dark photography.

Don't

  • Recolor, outline, drop-shadow or skew the wordmark.
  • Place the blue logo on a dark or busy background.
  • Stretch the aspect ratio or rotate the mark.
  • Add a tagline directly under the wordmark in another font.
