05 — Fonts

Typography

Titillium Web for everything structural — titles, subtitles, headings, section labels. Epilogue for everything you actually read — body, quote, caption. Both are free on Google Fonts.

Titillium Web — headings

The structural typeface. Always bold for titles and subtitles; regular for the lower heading levels.

Epilogue — body & quote

The reading typeface. Regular for body, italic for pull-quotes, regular at 14 for captions.

BodyEpilogue · 16 · Regular
Body — we accelerate analytics and AI roadmaps with strategy, projects and experts that deliver real ROI.
QuoteEpilogue · 16 · Italic
"Plainsight turned our scattered data sources into a single, trustworthy platform in less than four months."
CaptionEpilogue · 14 · Regular
Caption — small supporting text under images, figures or footnotes.

Fallbacks

When Titillium Web or Epilogue can't load (offline Word, some legacy email clients), fall back to Arial — close enough proportions to keep layouts intact. Never substitute with Times, Calibri or Helvetica.

/* CSS font stack */
--font-head: "Titillium Web", "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif;
--font-body: "Epilogue", "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif;

Where to get the fonts

  • Titillium Web — fonts.google.com/specimen/Titillium+Web. Free, open-source. Install weights 400 / 600 / 700.
  • Epilogue — fonts.google.com/specimen/Epilogue. Free, open-source. Install weights 400 / 500 / 600 / 700.
